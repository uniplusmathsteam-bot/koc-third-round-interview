S3 Mathematics Topic: Inequality · set Deliverable: HTML

An interactive tool for Inequality.

Build a browser tool that teaches one idea from Inequality—solving a linear inequality and reading the solution off a number line, including the moment the sign flips because both sides were divided by a negative. The student must be able to change something, commit to an answer, and be told whether it is right and why.

Learning goal

After using your tool, a student should be able to solve a linear inequality and know when the inequality sign turns around—and be corrected by the tool when they get it wrong.

◈ The topic is set, the teaching is yours Inequality is fixed for this task. How you teach it—number line, balance scale, sorting game—is your decision, and that decision is what we mark.

  • AlternativeA tester: given one inequality, the student drags values onto a number line and the tool says which ones satisfy it, and why the boundary point is or is not included.

05 · What matters

Show judgement, not perfection.

We are looking for a future KOC: someone who can turn subject knowledge into useful, engaging teaching material, work several lanes at once, and explain the choices behind it.

What we are not marking

Three things candidates worry about that carry no marks at all.

  • Not markedWhether you can write code yourself. You are expected to direct the AI, not to type Python.
  • Not markedPolish and animation flourish. A plain piece that teaches beats a beautiful one that misleads.
  • Not markedInstallation trouble. If Manim will not install, say so and demonstrate what you have.

◈ Unfinished is acceptable Bring an incomplete piece and explain what was left and why. A candidate who knows what is wrong with their work scores better than one who does not notice.
