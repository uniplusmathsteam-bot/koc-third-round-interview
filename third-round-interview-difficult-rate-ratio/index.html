YOUR STARTING POINT Live · the same file is in your folder

A finished tool with a hole in it.

It teaches rate and ratio properly: a mountain journey with a clock and a speed the student chooses, a cake the student cuts into slices to make a ratio, and a worked LCM method for joining a : b = 2 : 5 with b : c = 3 : 7. Then it stops — some way short of the revision set.

  • What it already doesTwo tabs, Rate and Ratio. Each one opens with a swipeable carousel of concept cards, then hands the student something to operate. Every formula is typeset through a single helper called renderTex().
  • What it already doesThe student commits before the reveal: you cut the cake, press Confirm, and only then does it print A : B, A : whole and B : whole underneath.
  • The gapSearch the file for the word “proportion”. It is not there — not once. JM16 spends five questions on direct and inverse proportion, and this tool teaches neither.
  • Also missingMap scale (1 : 500 000), the phrase “simplest form” with H.C.F., and the JM16 questions where a ratio changes after some items are taken away.

The three things your change list must say

Two or three sentences each. Write in English or Chinese, whichever is faster for you.

  1. 1 · What the tool already teachesList what a student can already do in the Rate tab and in the Ratio tab. If you cannot say this, you will spend the hour rebuilding something that is already there.

Getting the mathematics right

This is where the difficult version is actually difficult. AI treats almost every pair of numbers as direct proportion and scales them — which is exactly the mistake JM16 is built to catch.

01Direct: x up, y up, and x₁/x₂ = y₁/y₂. Inverse: x up, y down, and x₁y₁ = x₂y₂. Check your tool has not swapped them.
02Check the trap in Q14: 6 workers make 6 carpets in 6 days. 12 workers in 6 days make 12; 12 workers in 12 days make 24.
03Check Q7 by hand: 240 kg in 6 boxes is 40 kg a box, so 8 boxes hold 320 kg — the claim of 300 kg is wrong, and the reason matters more than the number.
04Check a scale: 1 : 500 000 means 1 cm on the map is 500 000 cm = 5 km, so 3.6 cm is 18 km.
05Work one QUE question by hand. If something is wrong and you cannot fix it in time, say so in the demonstration — we mark honesty higher than a hidden error.

06 · What matters

Show judgement, not perfection.

We are looking for a future KOC: someone who can pick up teaching material that already exists, see what it does not cover, and improve it without damaging it. Most of the work we give you will be this shape.

What we are not marking

Three things candidates worry about that carry no marks at all.

  • Not markedWhether you can write code yourself. You are expected to direct the AI, not to type JavaScript.
  • Not markedHow much you added. One section that fits beats four half-finished ones.
  • Not markedPolish and animation flourish. A plain new tab that handles inverse proportion correctly beats a beautiful one that scales it like direct proportion.

◈ Unfinished is acceptable — unnoticed is not Bring an incomplete change and tell us what is left and why. “My Confirm button still accepts an empty answer” scores better than not having noticed. Breaking the Rate tab and not noticing scores worst of all.
